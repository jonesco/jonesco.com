Buy Sell Hold for iPhone and iPad

Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026. Updated November 14, 2026.

Buy Sell Hold does not operate user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or a developer-controlled server.

Information stored on your device

Your watchlist, price thresholds, preferences, tutorial status, and imported backup data are stored locally on your device using Apple system storage. Your Finnhub API key is stored in the iOS Keychain. Buy Sell Hold’s developer does not receive this information.

Finnhub

If you connect Finnhub, the app sends requests directly from your device to Finnhub. Those requests contain your Finnhub API key and the stock symbols you request. Finnhub processes that information under its own terms, including its privacy provisions. Buy Sell Hold is not affiliated with or endorsed by Finnhub.

Google links

If you choose to open a stock-information link, the app opens a Google search in your browser. Google and your browser process that activity under their respective privacy policies. Buy Sell Hold does not receive the search or its results.

Backups

When you export a watchlist backup, you choose where the JSON file is saved or shared. The file may include symbols, company names, prices, thresholds, and timestamps. Buy Sell Hold does not receive exported files.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete individual entries, clear your Finnhub key, replace imported data, or delete the app. Data sent to Finnhub or Google is governed by those services’ retention policies.

Children

Buy Sell Hold is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app’s practices change. The dates above identify the latest version.
