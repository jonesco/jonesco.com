Artifactr for iPhone, iPad, and Mac

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Artifactr is a personal library for the prompts, snippets, and ideas you save. Artifactr does not collect, sell, rent, share, or use your data for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Information you create

Artifact titles, text, images, categories, tags, favorites, version history, and timestamps are stored on your device. When iCloud is available, this content may sync through your private iCloud database so it remains available across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Jonesco Worldwide, LLC does not receive or have access to the content in your private iCloud database.

Version history

When you save an edit, Artifactr keeps the earlier version of that artifact so you can review and revert it. Earlier versions are stored and synced the same way as the artifact itself.

Backups and sharing

Artifactr can export and import JSON backups, and can share an artifact as text or as a JSON file. Backups and shared items go only to the location or app you choose. Artifactr does not receive them, and they remain where you put them until you move or delete them.

Search and preferences

Searches happen on your device. Artifactr does not transmit search terms. Basic app preferences, such as your appearance setting and accent color, remain on your device.

Deletion and retention

Deleting an artifact removes it from Artifactr and, when iCloud sync is active, syncs that deletion across your devices. Deleting a category does not delete the artifacts in it. Uninstalling Artifactr may not delete data already stored in your private iCloud account. Exported backup files remain until you delete them.

Children, advertising, and tracking

Artifactr has no advertising, third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, user accounts, or developer-operated server. The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If Artifactr’s privacy practices change, this page will be updated before those changes take effect.
