Buy Sell Hold for iPhone and iPad

Support

Buy Sell Hold is a personal watchlist and threshold tool for iPhone and iPad. It does not provide financial advice or execute trades. If something is not working as expected, email wes@jonesco.com .

Connecting Finnhub

  1. Create your personal account at Finnhub.
  2. Copy your Finnhub API key.
  3. Open Buy Sell Hold and paste the key on the connection screen.
  4. Select Test & Continue.

Your key is stored in the iOS Keychain. Buy Sell Hold does not operate a shared key or a developer-controlled quote server.

Troubleshooting

  • Confirm that the key has no leading or trailing spaces.
  • Check your internet connection and Finnhub account status.
  • In the app, open Settings > API Keys to test, replace, or clear the key.
  • Quotes can be delayed, incomplete, or temporarily unavailable.

What to include when contacting support

Please include your device and iOS version, what you were trying to do, and what happened. Do not include your Finnhub API key or other sensitive information.
